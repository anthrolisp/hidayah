# Privacy Policy — Hidayah

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

**Hidayah** ("the app") is a reference guide for new Muslims, beginning with the Islamic ablution and prayer. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It works entirely on your device.

## What the app stores

The app saves two small preferences **on your device only**, using local storage:

- Your chosen color theme (light or dark)
- Your chosen text size

These never leave your device, are not transmitted anywhere, and are not associated with any identity. You can clear them at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect names, emails, accounts, or contact information.
- It does not track your location.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
- It does not require an account or login.
- It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

## Network use

Once installed, the app functions fully offline. The bundled version contains all text and images locally and makes no network requests for its core content. (If you use the web version, your browser may load fonts from Google Fonts; the installed app versions bundle or cache these and do not depend on a network connection.)

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
